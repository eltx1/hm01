This is a code-evidence audit, not a roadmap restatement. A migration, model, permission, route, service, view label, or documentation claim is not treated as a complete capability unless the usable workflow can be traced across the applicable layers.

## Executive summary

Horus Media already has a substantial, coherent AdTech control-plane core. The strongest productized areas are authentication, publisher onboarding, site lifecycle and verification, GAM connection routing, permanent-loader/static delivery, Prebid, optional native demand, direct campaigns, and the underlying reporting/finance calculations. The fixed architecture is consistently preserved: `HORUS_GAM` defaults are enforced in migrations and lifecycle services; publishers keep one permanent loader; ad requests stay out of Laravel; only aggregated reporting reaches the application; and production remains cron-based PHP/MySQL with precompiled browser assets.

The largest risk is not missing domain logic. It is the gap between implemented backend capabilities and an operable control center:

1. **Financial operations are not safely productized.** `Admin\ReportingController` exposes routes for report connections, imports, revenue rules, adjustments, period closing, and publisher payments, but `resources/views/admin/reporting/index.blade.php` is almost entirely read-only. More importantly, `RevenueAdjustmentService` and `PublisherPaymentService` do not enforce four-eyes separation, and payment settlement does not require an approved payment state.
2. **Several seeded permissions do not control a product surface.** `dashboard.*`, `users.view`, `audit.view`, `finance.publisher.view`, `finance.internal_margin.view`, and `support.manage` are not enforced by a corresponding route/workflow. `/` selects a dashboard by organization type without checking the seeded dashboard permission. `RolePermissionController` can attach any system role to any user without validating role/organization compatibility.
3. **Audit coverage is broad but incomplete where operational evidence matters most.** Report-connection create/update/status operations and queue retry/forget do not create dedicated audit events. `audit.view` has no searchable audit-log route or UI.
4. **Supply-chain generation exists, but compliance management does not.** `SupplyChainArtifactBuilder`, static delivery, daily `supply-chain:check`, `DemandAdsTxtRecord`, `SellerDeclaration`, and schain payload generation must be reused. What is missing is a cross-site ads.txt compliance workbench, publisher remediation UX, sellers.json lifecycle governance, schain validation, and visible check history.
5. **Control-plane navigation is fragmented.** Every Blade page supplies its own `@section('navigation')`; there is no authoritative role-aware shell. Admin publisher/site pages are thin list/detail screens rather than Publisher 360 and Site 360 workbenches. Several existing actions (inventory sync, config rollback, report import, payment lifecycle) are therefore buried or unreachable from UI.
6. **Notifications and support are placeholders.** The only domain notification is `UserInvitationNotification`; no site review, verification failure, contract, report import, statement, payment, campaign, demand, GAM-health, supply-compliance, or operational alert notification exists. `support.manage` has no ticket model, controller, route, UI, or test.

### Immediate disposition

- Do **not** rebuild GAM routing, loader delivery, Prebid, demand connectors, campaign deployment, reporting calculations, statements, or payout calculations.
- Complete Task 1 on top of the existing routes/services, then Tasks 2–4 on top of the existing supply-chain builder and static delivery pipeline.
- Before live finance use, close P0 authorization, cross-organization, audit, and payment-state gaps listed below.
