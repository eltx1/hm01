Privacy Policy

This Policy explains how HorusMedia handles personal information across our websites, dashboards, advertising services, publisher tools, and business communications.

Effective date: August 22, 2026 · Version: 2026-08-22

Advertising technology involves several independent participants. Publishers, advertisers, platforms, and partners may each have their own privacy responsibilities and policies.

1. Scope, entities, and roles

HorusMedia operates through Horus Media LLC, 30 N Gould St Ste N, Sheridan, WY 82801, US, and HORUS MEDIA GROUP LTD, 2 Frederick Street, Kings Cross, London, United Kingdom, WC1X 0ND. The responsible entity is the company identified in your account, agreement, invoice, or communication.

This Policy applies when that HorusMedia entity determines how personal information is used. In some services, we process information for a customer or partner under its instructions; that organization’s notice and agreement also apply. This Policy does not replace the privacy notices of third-party websites, applications, or advertising partners.

2. Information we collect

Account and business information

We may collect names, business contact details, organization and role information, login and security records, billing and tax information, domain or application ownership evidence, and support communications.

Service and device information

We may collect IP address, browser and device characteristics, timestamps, pages or features used, approximate location derived from IP, referral information, log events, cookie or similar identifiers, and diagnostic or security signals.

Advertising and transaction information

Depending on the service and applicable permissions, we may process ad request and delivery events, campaign and placement identifiers, consent signals, contextual information, coarse geography, auction data, performance events, traffic-quality signals, and reporting or payment records. We do not ask partners to provide information prohibited by law or platform policy.

3. How we use information

  • Provide, authenticate, operate, support, and secure the services.
  • Review publisher applications, advertising campaigns, integrations, domains, and traffic quality.
  • Deliver, measure, optimize, reconcile, and report advertising and monetization activity.
  • Detect fraud, invalid traffic, misuse, security incidents, and policy violations.
  • Process payments, invoices, taxes, disputes, and contractual obligations.
  • Communicate about accounts, service changes, support, and relevant business services.
  • Comply with law and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
  • Analyze and improve reliability, usability, and performance.

4. Legal grounds

Where a legal ground is required, we rely as appropriate on performance of a contract, legitimate interests such as service security and business operations, compliance with legal obligations, or consent. The applicable ground depends on the service, jurisdiction, and context. Consent may be withdrawn where processing relies on it, without affecting earlier lawful processing.

5. Cookies and advertising technology

We and authorized providers may use cookies, local storage, pixels, tags, SDKs, or server-side logs for authentication, security, preferences, measurement, and advertising operations. Availability of choices depends on the site, device, partner configuration, and applicable law. Publishers using HorusMedia technology must operate an appropriate consent or choice mechanism where required and pass valid consent signals to the platform.

6. How information is shared

We may share information with infrastructure, security, analytics, payment, professional-adviser, and support providers; with advertising demand and supply partners as needed to transact and measure advertising; with customers or account administrators; in a corporate transaction; or with authorities when legally required. Providers are permitted to use information only for agreed services, subject to applicable contracts and law.

We do not sell account contact information for unrelated third-party marketing. Advertising-related data sharing may be legally defined as a “sale,” “sharing,” or targeted advertising in some jurisdictions; applicable notices and choices will be provided where required.

7. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as needed for the purposes described, including account operation, reporting, payment, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, contractual obligations, and legal retention. Periods vary by data type and relationship. We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

8. International processing

HorusMedia and its providers or partners may process information in countries other than where it was collected. Where required, we use recognized transfer mechanisms and contractual or organizational protections appropriate to the transfer.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on location, individuals may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of personal information, and to appeal or complain to a regulator. Requests may require identity verification and can be limited by applicable exceptions. Account holders can update certain information in the service. Send privacy requests to the contact below and describe the account or relationship involved.

10. Children

The services are intended for businesses and people with legal capacity to enter commercial relationships. They are not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through account registration.

11. Changes

We may update this Policy to reflect service, legal, or operational changes. The effective date identifies the current version. Material changes may also be communicated through the service or by another appropriate channel.

12. Contact
